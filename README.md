# 時隙鐘聲 The Bell Between Hours

An original time-travel RPG in Chinese and English. Play it at https://ywchen0711.github.io/bell-between-hours/

Ren, a clockmaker's apprentice in AD 1250, helps finish a great bell with a heart of star-iron. When the Queen rings it,
the town freezes and Ren is thrown eleven centuries forward into Stillfall, a ruined world where the bell has been stopping
time for a thousand years. With Mira the time scholar, Silas the scavenger and Kaya the huntress from 3000 BC, Ren crosses
three eras to tune the bell.

But the Hush they silence in the Silent Spire is only a shadow. Ebb the Lamplighter leads them to the **Hour Between**, a
place outside every era, and its heart sleeps past the end of time behind a door with three locks. The keys lie in three
more ages: **Brasshaven, AD 1820**, where a grieving professor builds a machine to make every clock agree; **Frostcradle,
30000 BC**, where Nilo the mammoth hunter's people sing the first star-iron to sleep; and **Emberfall, AD 9000**, under a
dying sun, where Vesper the Bellwright must face the machine she built to hold on to her sister.

- **Battles happen on the map.** Walk into monsters you can see, and the party spreads out to fight right there.
- **Active time gauges.** Each friend acts when their gauge fills; the game waits while you choose.
- **Where people stand matters.** Techniques reach one foe, a line, a circle or everyone, and the ground they would hit is marked as you aim.
- **Combined techniques.** 26 two- and three-person combos across six heroes.
- **Seven ages, six heroes, 79 kinds of monster (20 bosses)** — bosses with their own rules: machines that wind up (thunder snaps the spring), ice armour that only fire melts, summoners, a foe whose weakness keeps changing.
- **Statuses and accessories:** haste, slow, shields, regeneration, freezing, break; accessories that stop poison or stuns, double gold, counter-attack, halve MP costs and more.
- **The Hour Between** is a hub with era travel, a forge that turns monster materials into gear, a monster book with rewards, a quest log, and an old fisherman.
- **Side stories for every hero**, each teaching a technique and giving a keepsake.
- **Paradoxes:** plant an acorn in 3000 BC and a great oak shelters Stillfall in 2400; open a floodgate in 1820 and a dead river runs again; save a mammoth calf in 30000 BC and its herd lives on in the Rootwild.
- **The Gearwork Fair:** a strength bell, clockwork beetle races and a prize booth; **fishing** at three spots.
- **Three endings**, New Game+ that keeps levels and items, and a post-game superboss behind the Unrung Bell.

Controls: arrows/WASD move, Z/Enter confirm, X back (hold to run), C/Tab menu, M music. Touch buttons appear on phones.
